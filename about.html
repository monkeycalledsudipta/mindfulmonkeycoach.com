My story

The obstacle is the way.

30Started training
31First push-up
34First front flip
Naked and AfraidFirst Indian woman finalist
Apex MovementCoach certification, ongoing

I have spent many years working through my own insecurity, pain, financial struggles, confusion and fear. I found my way back to myself through play, movement, self-care, nature and joy.

As a woman, I understand that women often need a different kind of space to feel safe, strong and seen. And I believe men, too, deserve spaces where they can soften, reconnect with themselves and receive the love and support they may have spent years learning to live without.

My mission is to bring people back to the joy of being alive through movement, play, mindful living and the lessons life has taught me. To create a space where women and men can explore their strength, embrace their vulnerability and come home to themselves, with greater freedom, clarity and self-trust.

Because I believe that life is not about avoiding obstacles or constantly trying to fix ourselves. It is about learning to move through life with courage, curiosity and a little more play.

The obstacle is the way. And sometimes, the way forward is to play.

My philosophy

Simple principles
for a complex life.

No quick fixes and no aesthetic ideals. Just practical ways to become stronger, steadier and more alive, for the long term.
