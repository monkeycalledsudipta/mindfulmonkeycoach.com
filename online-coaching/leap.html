Questions

Good to know.

I have no fitness routine. Is this for me?

Yes. LEAP is built around where you are today, not where you think you should be.

I don't intend to become an athlete. Is this for me?

Yes. Most clients simply want to move, feel and function better in everyday life.

What if I miss or need to reschedule a class?

Give at least 12 hours' notice. See the refund and cancellation policy for full details.

What equipment do I need?

Very little. Sudipta will tell you exactly what you need for your setup during onboarding.

Is there a refund policy?

Fees are non-refundable once a programme begins.

What if I have a current or past injury?

Tell Sudipta upfront. The programme is adapted around your injury history.

What commitment are you asking for?

Four months of live 1-on-1 sessions twice a week, plus daily movement and nutrition practice.
